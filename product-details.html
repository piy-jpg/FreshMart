About Fresh Desi Tomato

Our Desi Tomatoes are grown using organic compost and natural farm practices across fertile plots in Kolar, Karnataka. Unlike industrial cold-storage varieties, our tomatoes are harvested strictly at dawn and dispatched within 4 hours to preserve their essential acidity, sweet pulp, and fragrant vine aroma.

Storage Recommendations

💡 Storage advice: Store in a cool and dry place. Refrigerate when appropriate.

  • Keep tomatoes stem-side down at room temperature (18°C–22°C) away from direct sunlight for the first 2 days to maintain complete aroma and flavor.
  • Once fully ripe and red, refrigerate in the crisper drawer in a breathable paper bag for up to 5 additional days.
  • Bring refrigerated tomatoes to room temperature 30 minutes before cooking or slicing for peak flavor.
